PREVIEW
Own the safehouse ($20,000) and add its fuel pump ($10,000): unload fuel tanks at its grid with T, take them with Y, hold G at the pump to fill from them. Press T at a gas pump to load or refill a fuel tank onto a pickup bed or flatbed trailer (hold G anywhere to refuel from it). Semis haul a box trailer (press E at its rear to open/close the doors - closing locks any vehicles inside). Pickups can tow trailers: back up to one and press Q to hitch or unhitch. Drive a car onto a car trailer, get out and press E to strap it down. White work trucks have a ram bumper - press L for the amber light. Smash trash cans and crates for loot. Climb ladders to roofs. Beware the zombies - an infection kills you and turns you into one.
